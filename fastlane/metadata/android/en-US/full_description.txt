<b>One tree, every source</b>

Browse local storage, archives, FTP, SFTP, SMB, WebDAV, S3, restic and Jellyfin/Emby. Copy or compress across sources. Archives expand in place.

<b>Small and resource-conscious</b>

The 1.18.0 libre APK is 10.88 MiB for arm64-v8a + x86_64, including terminal and media player.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>Single/dual panes; cross-directory/range/pattern selection; pinned or independent branches; reorder roots, favorites, websites and actions; OLED theme, folder counts, Unix permissions and per-folder views</li>
<li>Opposite-pane copy/move targets; optional directory structure for transfers/compression; background transfers, retry and resume; local trash with batch restore/purge</li>
<li>Dual-pane file picking and shared file/text saving; cache remote selections and shares</li>
<li>Batch rename with text/regex replacement, numbering and previews</li>
<li>Encrypted ZIP/7z, create tar/tar.gz, Extract to… dialog, faster ZIP/7z batch copies, stream tar.gz/tar.xz/tar.bz2/tar.zst</li>
<li>File comparison and cross-source folder comparison/sync</li>
<li>SQLite tables in the tree: search, sort, filter, read-only SQL, BLOB hex previews and CSV export</li>
<li>Disk usage treemap with pinch zoom and scanned sizes/usage bars in the tree; bottom toolbar/address bar, Back navigation/exit choices, completion vibration and foreground notifications</li>
<li>Private space excluded from backups and configuration exports; files are deleted when Twig is uninstalled</li>
<li>Text editing with syntax highlighting and per-file encoding choices; external editing of writable local/SAF documents; hex, PDF (passwords on Android 15+), SVG, image and media viewers; GIF/animated WebP on Android 9+; image selection sync; subtitle size/color and video control auto-hide</li>
<li>Music playlists, M3U imports, lyrics and background playback</li>
<li>Git status, history, side-by-side diff, worktrees and submodules for local and remote repositories</li>
<li>Local, SSH and privileged terminals with sessions, idle dimming and shell-directory navigation</li>
<li>File, folder, website and terminal shortcuts; path buttons and automatic tree indentation; long-press actions for browsing, music and terminals</li>
<li>Wi-Fi sharing: Twig becomes an HTTP and WebDAV server, so a desktop can mount it</li>
<li>Website bookmarks, home-screen shortcuts, per-site settings, JS/CSS, element hiding, resource blocking and debugging; configurable web search and shared domain settings</li>
<li>Website downloads and background sessions</li>
<li>Root/Shizuku access within the local tree; Android/data access through saved SAF grants where Android permits it</li>
<li>Encrypted saved credentials, master-password app lock, fingerprint unlock and configuration backups</li>
</ul>

<b>Less usual things</b>

<ul>
<li>Read-only restic backups decrypted on-device, with snapshots as dated folders and recent snapshot locations.</li>
<li>Videos recognized as a series share remembered opening/ending skip marks; Continue Watching retains the next episode.</li>
<li>Network MP4/MKV thumbnails fetch keyframes through container indexes.</li>
<li>Blu-ray M2TS, DTS-HD Master Audio, TrueHD AC-3 cores and PGS subtitles.</li>
<li>Jellyfin/Emby playback progress syncs to the server; posters, tags, lyrics and external subtitles come from its API.</li>
</ul>

<b>About this build</b>

F-Droid distributes the free-software <i>libre</i> build without RAR. The developer’s <i>full</i> build adds read-only RAR through junrar, whose UnRAR license has non-free restrictions. Other features are identical. A separate full arm64 APK adds optional NFSv3/v4 support.

Twig has no ads, analytics or telemetry. Network access follows your actions. Opened websites may load ads or trackers.

Optional microphone, camera and location permissions require your consent for each website and are configured separately. Saved bookmarks can remember decisions; temporary pages always ask.
