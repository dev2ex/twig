A built-in PDF reader: continuous or single-page scrolling, double-tap to crop margins, text selection, and full-text search on Android 15+. Hex compare reads two files side by side, byte for byte. Split-APK bundles (.xapk/.apks/.apkm) install directly. SMB lists every share when the name is left blank, and FTP/SFTP/S3 connections take a start path. GB18030 replaces GBK in the text encoding list. Fixed: AVI audio and frame order, 4K playback exhausting memory, git worktrees over SSH.
