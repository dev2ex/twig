The build no longer embeds Android Gradle Plugin's dependency metadata in the APK. That block is an inventory of the libraries used, encrypted so that only Google can read it, and it exists for Play Console's vulnerability scanning; in a build distributed anywhere else it is 5.8 KB of content nobody can review. Removing it is also what lets F-Droid accept the APK, since their scanner rejects opaque extra blocks in the signing area.
