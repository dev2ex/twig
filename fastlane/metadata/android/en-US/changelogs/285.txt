New archive formats: tar, and gz/xz/bz2/zst as single-file compression. A .tar.gz opens to the tar inside it and expands again, so the whole tar.* family works, including the .tgz/.txz/.tbz2/.tzst spellings. Entries in a tar are read in place, so an archive nested inside one opens without being unpacked first and video inside it can seek. Where a container does not record the original size, the size is now left blank instead of showing 0 B.
